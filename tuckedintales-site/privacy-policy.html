Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At TuckedIn Tales, we know you're trusting us with something precious — a photo of your child. We take that seriously, and this page explains exactly what we do (and don't do) with your information.

What we collect

  • Your child's name, age, and gender (used only to personalize the story)
  • A photo of your child, uploaded to create the illustrations
  • Your delivery address and phone number, to ship the finished book
  • Payment details are handled entirely by Razorpay — we never see or store your card or UPI information

How we use it

Your child's photo and details are used solely to create and deliver your personalized storybook. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Parental consent & AI processing

Our service is for parents and legal guardians. By uploading a photo and placing an order, you confirm that you are the child's parent or legal guardian (or have the parent or guardian's permission), and you give your consent for your child's photo and details to be processed, including by a third-party AI image service, solely to create your child's personalized storybook. If you are not the child's parent or legal guardian, please do not place an order.

You can withdraw this consent at any time by emailing tuckedintales.12@gmail.com. Once an order has been illustrated or printed, we may not be able to undo that work, but we will stop using the photo and delete it from our systems.

Photo storage & security

Uploaded photos are stored in a private, access-controlled system. They are not publicly accessible, and are only viewed by the team members involved in illustrating your book. Photos are processed by a third-party AI image service solely to create your illustrations, and once your order has been fulfilled we delete the photo from our systems.

Emails we send you

We use your email address to send your order confirmation and updates as your book is illustrated, printed and shipped. These emails are sent through a third-party email service provider, which only receives your name, email address and the order details in the message. We do not send marketing emails without your permission.

Website analytics

We may use basic website analytics (for example Google Analytics) to count visits and see which pages are used. It does not receive your child's photo or any details from your order.

Marketing use of photos

We will never use your child's photo or story details in our marketing, on our website, or on social media without asking you directly first.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the data we hold about your order, or request deletion of your child's photo after your order is complete, by emailing us at tuckedintales.12@gmail.com.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach out at tuckedintales.12@gmail.com.
