📸 Tips for a great photo
Do
  • ✅ Clear, front-facing face
  • ✅ Daylight or bright, even light
  • ✅ Eyes open, natural expression
  • ✅ Only your child in the photo
  • ✅ Close enough to see the face well
Avoid
  • ❌ Sunglasses, hats or masks
  • ❌ Filters, stickers or heavy editing
  • ❌ Blurry or dark photos
  • ❌ Group photos
  • ❌ Side or far-away shots
