Questions Parents Ask ❓

Everything you need to know before ordering.

How long does delivery take?+

Most storybooks are illustrated, printed, and delivered within 7–10 days across India.

What if my child's photo isn't clear?+

We recommend a well-lit, front-facing photo without sunglasses or hats. If we run into any issue using your photo, we'll reach out to you before starting illustration — you won't be left guessing.

Can I make changes after placing an order?+

Reach out within 24 hours of ordering and we'll do our best to update the name, theme, or details before illustration begins.

Is payment on this site secure?+

Yes — all payments are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway used by thousands of Indian businesses. We never see or store your card details.

What if I'm not happy with the book?+

If the illustration quality isn't right, we'll redo it free of charge. See our Refund Policy for full details.

Do you deliver across all of India?+

Yes, we deliver pan-India. Delivery charges and timelines are shown at checkout based on your pincode.
